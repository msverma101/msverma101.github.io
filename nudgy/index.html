Nudgy

Nudgy is a free, open-source desktop companion for Linux. It gathers your tasks, calendar events and important unread mail into one short list, and shows what needs you now.

What it does with your Google account

  • Google Calendar and Google Tasks: shows your upcoming events and open tasks in the list.
  • Gmail: shows the sender and subject of important or starred unread mail. If you ask for a reply draft, it reads that one message. Nudgy never sends, deletes or marks mail as read.
  • Adding events and tasks: only when you ask, with a separate permission you grant the first time.

Your data is stored on your own computer. There is no Nudgy server.
