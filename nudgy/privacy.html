Nudgy privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Nudgy runs on your own computer. Its developer has no server for it, collects no usage data and cannot see your data.

Google data Nudgy reads

  • Calendar and Tasks (read-only): upcoming events and open tasks.
  • Gmail (read-only): your address, and the sender, subject and labels of important or starred unread mail and of LinkedIn notification mail. When you ask for a reply draft, the text of that one message.
  • Calendar events and Tasks (write): a separate permission, requested the first time you ask Nudgy to add something. Nudgy uses it only to add what you asked for and to undo that.

How it is used

Only to show, rank and remind you of your items, and to write drafts or steps you ask for. It is never sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Where it is stored

In files on your computer that only your user account can read. Sign-in tokens are stored there too.

When data leaves your computer

  • To Google, to fetch your items or add what you asked for.
  • Language models run on your computer by default. Only if you connect Claude, the text needed for a job you allow (an item's title and details, the list of open items for ranking, or a message's text for a draft) is sent to Anthropic under your own account.
  • If you turn on the focus check, Nudgy may look up an unfamiliar term from a window title on Wikipedia and, if you connected Claude, send the window's title and app, with the titles of up to five urgent items, to Anthropic. A window title can contain what you have open, such as a mail subject.
  • If you share your list with your phone, it is written to the folder you choose; the tool you use to sync that folder carries it.

Nudgy also contacts other services you enable, such as event feeds. They are not sent your Google data.

Deleting your data

Run ./uninstall.sh --purge to delete Nudgy's settings, data and sign-ins. Copies in a phone-sync folder you chose must be deleted there. You can withdraw Google access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google's policy

Nudgy's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact
